Methodology notes
- Read bench seeds 5000 docs, closes the DB, reopens, then iterates the
  entire collection per op. After the first iteration the per-connection
  pcache is warm, so subsequent ops measure mostly cache-hit reads (codec
  cost is paid on initial cache-fill, not per row). This reflects steady-
  state behavior of a long-running DB.
- Insert bench uses a fresh DB per b.N=1 dispatch; codec runs on every
  page write that flushes through to WAL.
- ChaCha20-Poly1305 has no AES-NI; on amd64/arm64 it goes through Go's
  pure-software path. AES-GCM uses the AES-NI / ARMv8 crypto extensions.

Bottom line
- IntegrityChecksum is the right default for paranoid users: zero
  measurable cost on reads, ~1% on writes, full corruption detection.
- AEAD modes give cryptographic guarantees with a 5-30% perf cost
  depending on operation mix and cipher choice.
